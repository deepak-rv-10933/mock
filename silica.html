The industry

How glass actually gets made.

Six stages, each with its own paperwork, approvals and handoffs.

01
Batching
Silica, soda ash, lime weighed and mixed
→
02
Melting
Furnace at 1,500°C, continuous feed
→
03
Forming
Float bath, rolling or blowing
→
04
Annealing
Controlled cool-down through the lehr
→
05
Inspection
Cutting, defect scan, tolerance check
→
06
Dispatch
Crating, load planning, delivery

Every stage generates a decision someone has to chase. That is where our agents sit.
