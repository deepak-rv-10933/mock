The setup

One owner.
Five apps.
Five versions of the truth.

Plants in Chennai, Pune and Vadodara. A region head in every market, a plant head on every floor. Every team lives in its own Zoho app, and each holds only one piece of the picture.

The question on the owner's desk
“Why is sales down this month?”
Zoho CRM
Sales: deals, accounts, approvals, churn signals.
Zoho Creator (MES)
The factory floor: production, downtime, stock, QC, dispatch.
Zoho Desk
The customer's voice: complaints & warranty claims.
Zoho Books
The money: invoices, receivables, margins.
Zoho Inventory
Movement: stock & warehouses across regions.
The blame cycle

Everyone's pointing. No one's connecting.

Each team is looking at its own app, and each is a little bit right. The real problem is the thread running through all of them, and no one can see the whole thread, so no one owns it.

The end-to-end journey

Twelve steps. Dozens of hands. One thread through it all.

From sand to a finished pane, the work crosses every team and every app: extraction, the furnace floor, quality, packaging, logistics. A small slip at any step ripples all the way to the customer, and no single screen shows the whole line.

The blind spot

The problem never lives in one app.

It hides in the seams between them: a small, forgivable signal in each, invisible as a whole. To catch it, someone would have to already suspect a question that spans five apps and five departments…

The question no one asks
“Which five small things, in five apps, are actually one problem?”

…so it never gets asked, and the problem compounds, quietly, until a customer walks.
